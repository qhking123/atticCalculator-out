Quick Answers

The five questions we hear most from homeowners, DIYers, and tradespeople.

How do I get a faster reply?▼

Include the exact inputs: your DOE climate zone number, gross attic square feet, roof pitch (rise/run), cutout area for chimneys and skylights, current insulation depth and material if any, and your target zone R-value. Add the bag count or cost you expected versus the one shown. That one message usually lets us reproduce and explain the difference in a single reply.

Do you offer live chat or phone support?▼

Not yet. Email lets us re-run the full attic calculation and verify every answer against the actual R-value math and the current DOE zone targets before responding — which matters when the topic is a $5,000 material order or a contractor quote. We read every message and answer most within two business days.

Can you tell me exactly what R-value I need?▼

We can tell you what the calculator's method says for the climate zone you enter — and show every formula behind it. But the zone targets are minimums; some homeowners go higher for exceptional comfort, and local energy codes may have different adopted R-values than the DOE recommends. Your local code official or a certified home energy rater makes the final call.

Do you sell insulation, blowers, or tools?▼

No. The site sells nothing, carries no affiliate links to Home Depot or Lowe's, and is not affiliated with any material manufacturer. Advertising is clearly labeled and separately handled — see the advertising section of the Privacy Policy for how that works.

Can I suggest a topic for the guide library?▼

Please do — that is how the library grows. Send the question you were trying to answer, the attic you were working on, and what you wished had been written down. Requests that describe a real job-site or workbench problem (e.g., 'how do I calculate R-value in a vented vs. unvented attic?') go to the top of the content queue.
