Why Penetrations Matter — The U-Value Gap

U-value is the mirror of R-value: U = 1/R. Higher R = better insulation (less heat flow). Lower U = better (less heat flow). Your R-49 attic insulation has U=0.020. A typical attic hatch has U=0.30. That means 15× more heat flows through each square foot of hatch than through the surrounding insulation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do attic penetrations cause heat loss?▼

Every penetration has a U-value — how easily heat passes through it — and surface area. Heat loss = U × A × ΔT. Your attic hatch might have U=0.30 (R-3.3), while your R-49 attic insulation has U=0.020. That's 15× more heat flowing per square foot through the hatch than through the insulation around it.

How big is the heat loss from a single hatch?▼

A standard 36×24 inch hatch (6 sq ft) with U=0.30 in an R-49 attic loses about 74 BTU/hr at 40°F temperature difference. Run that 2000 heating hours per year = 148,000 BTU/year. That's $5–$15/year in electricity or $2–$7/year in natural gas — from one hatch alone.

What's 'equivalent missing insulation'?▼

It translates heat loss into how many square feet of proper insulation you'd need to add to get the same savings. Formula: Equivalent sq ft = Penetration heat loss ÷ (U_attic_insulation × ΔT). A single hatch can cost you the performance of 150 sq ft of missing insulation — a powerful visualization.

How do I fix each penetration?▼

Attic hatch: rigid XPS foam board (1 inch = R-5) + weatherstripping → drops U to 0.20. Skylight: caulk curb + add attic tent (removable foam cover). Chimney: 2-inch mineral wool insulation around chase + caulk gaps. Attic door: replace with R-10+ door + weatherstrip all edges.

Why don't I just deduct cutout area from my insulation purchase?▼

Cutout deduction (which our Quick Mode handles) is about buying the right amount of material. This calculator is about how much energy you're wasting because of the U-value difference. They're complementary: deduction gets you the right amount, this calculator tells you why you should care about sealing.

How accurate is this calculator?▼

U-values are taken from standard ASTM testing for typical products. Your actual loss depends on your specific hatch/skylight model, how well it's weatherstripped, and your climate's actual heating degree days. Use it for budgeting and prioritizing — the exact dollar figure will vary, but the ranking (hatch > skylight > chimney) will be consistent.
